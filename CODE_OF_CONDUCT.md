# Code of Conduct

Participants in UTHARNESS OS are expected to communicate with respect, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and protect the privacy of local workspaces and credentials. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, malicious disclosure of secrets, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe. Concerns should be raised privately through the repository’s security or maintainer contact channel.
